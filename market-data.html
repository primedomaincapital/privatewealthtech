PrivateWealthTech.com Market Data

Wealth firms now spend millions a year on technology per firm, and the market estimates for the sector disagree with each other by a factor of three. Both facts matter.

Ask a room of wealth executives what their technology stack costs and you will get a precise answer. Ask the research industry how big wealth technology is and you will get a spread: $5.21 billion for 2024 from one firm, $18.32 billion from another. The gap is scope. Narrow definitions count platforms and software; broad ones sweep in services, advice delivery, and adjacent fintech revenue. This page shows both, labeled, because hiding the disagreement would be worse than reporting it.

The spending figures inside firms are less contested. Cerulli Associates, surveying large RIAs, found average technology spending projected at $3.3 million per firm, up from $2.9 million, with AI-specific spending set to more than double to $494,000 per firm from $237,000. The firms surveyed averaged $18 billion in assets under management — large firms, in other words, spending real money to keep advisors productive rather than to replace them.

That last point shows up in the hiring data too. Nearly three-quarters of surveyed RIAs expected to add junior advisors over the next two years. Technology, in this market, is a recruiting tool as much as an efficiency tool: 57% of advisors who recently joined a broker-dealer said technology influenced the move.

Reading the numbers

The funding collapse and the spending growth are not a contradiction. Venture funding into WealthTech fell 65% in the U.S. in 2024 while incumbent firms raised their own technology budgets — a rotation from startup bets to in-house build and vendor consolidation, not a retreat from technology.

Read the two market-size series as bookends, not as a trend line. They come from different firms with different definitions, and joining them into one growth curve would manufacture a precision neither source claims.

Methodology

Included: public research summaries of market size (two scopes, both labeled), Cerulli survey spending figures as reported by Barron's and Connect Money, and FinTech Global funding totals. Excluded: paywalled Cerulli report detail, vendor press-release revenue claims that could not be cross-checked, and any figure behind a login or paywall. Figures were chosen to show both the market's size and what firms actually spend.

Disclaimer.
